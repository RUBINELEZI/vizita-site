Questions

Before you ask.

Does it replace my sales reps?

No. It does the part reps have no time for: the first message, the follow-ups and finding a slot. The video call, the treatment plan and the sale stay with your consultants.

Which WhatsApp number does it use?

A new company number on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, in your own Meta business account. Your reps keep their own numbers. After booking, the patient gets their consultant's number.

Which languages does it speak?

Italian, English, German and Albanian for the pilot. It picks the language from the lead's country and phone number, and follows the lead if they switch.

What if a patient asks something medical?

It doesn't give medical advice or comment on x-rays. It thanks the patient, saves the files for the consultant, and alerts a person.

Where is patient data stored?

On servers in the EU. Photos and x-rays stay in the CRM instead of on staff phones, and are deleted after a period you set.

How much does it cost after the pilot?

A small monthly platform fee plus a fee per booked call, with unlimited users. Founding clinics keep their launch price.
